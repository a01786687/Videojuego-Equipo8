How to Play

01

Explore the Swamp

Navigate through platform style generated swamp levels. Collect mosquitoes, attack enemies with your tongue and defeat enemies along the way.

02

Enter the Cave

Find the cave entrance to begin the boss fight. Each boss has unique attack patterns.

03

Death & Cards

When you die, you reach the Card Selection Screen. Spend your mosquitoes to buy one new card, then start a fresh run stronger than before.

04

Keep Going

Mosquitoes never reset on death. Every run makes you stronger. Keep trying, experiment with cards, and climb the food chain.

The Card System

Cards are abilities that modify how your frog plays. You have three slots, one per category. Cards are single use, when activated they are burned and replaced by the next card in your deck.

1. MOVEMENT

Mobility and traversal abilities

2. COMBAT

Offensive and defensive abilities

3. UTILITY

Support and resource abilities
